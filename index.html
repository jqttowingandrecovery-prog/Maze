DARK MAZE

You are in a pitch-black maze, and something is hunting you. Send out a scan and it paints everything around you, in every direction at once. The dots stay and slowly dim, so you build a map as you go.

Every scan is loud: the monster hears it and heads for the spot you scanned from. It only shows up for a few seconds when your scan hits it, so listen for the heartbeat. Sprinting is fast but tiring, and the monster notices you from farther away while you do it. The gold pillar is your way out, but it has no power: find and start the generator first.

Crouching is slow and quiet: if the monster can't see you (a corner or wall is in the way) it stops and waits. In a straight line of sight it keeps coming, and a scan still calls it. Every 10 seconds it shows itself for a moment, wherever it is, even through walls. You won't enjoy looking at it.

The generator shows up orange on a scan. Stand next to it for about a second to start it. It is loud, and the monster will come for it. The radar (on your left hand in VR, in the corner on phone and desktop) shows how far away your goal is: the generator first, then the exit.

VR: trigger = scan  ·  left stick = move  ·  click the stick = sprint  ·  right stick = snap turn  ·  A/X or B/Y (or physically crouch) = crouch
Phone: left side = move, drag right side = look, SCAN button, SPRINT and CROUCH buttons (tap on / off)
Desktop: WASD move  ·  Shift sprint  ·  hold C or Ctrl (or tap X) crouch  ·  mouse look  ·  click or Space = scan  ·  Esc = menu
